## 3. Traditional Precedent

Dexy’s value should not be confused with “signal selling.” The closest traditional analogs are:

- risk analytics
- transaction cost analysis
- performance attribution
- execution analytics

Representative references:

- [Bloomberg Portfolio & Risk Analytics](https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/product/portfolio-risk-analytics/)
- [Bloomberg BTCA](https://professional.bloomberg.com/products/trading/post-trade-services/btca/)

Why it matters:

## 4. Market Floor and Ceiling Logic

### Floor

As a narrow operator SaaS, the floor is supported by:

- subscription benchmarks in onchain analytics
- repeat usage patterns for risk and wallet monitoring
- the possibility of serving a small but high-value user base

### Ceiling

The ceiling expands when the product evolves from:

- dashboard

to:

- workflow
- integration layer
- eventually flow-linked monetization

This is the critical commercial distinction for Dexy.
