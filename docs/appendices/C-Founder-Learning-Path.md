# Appendix C: Founder Learning Path

This appendix preserves the informal founder learning track that came out of our discussion. It is intentionally plain-language.

## Goal

The founder does not need to become a top Hyperliquid trader.

The founder needs to become someone who:

1. can read a Hyperliquid wallet
2. understands what an operator actually fears
3. can separate risk, leakage, and explanation problems

## 7-Day Track

### Day 1: vocabulary

Learn the plain-language meaning of:

- perp
- leverage
- margin
- liquidation
- funding
- maker / taker
- fill
- slippage
- unrealized vs realized PnL
- vault
- subaccount

### Day 2: user workflow

Internalize the simple path:

- see market
- choose direction
- set size and leverage
- place order
- fill
- hold position
- risk changes
- close / liquidate / continue

### Day 3: wallet observation

Watch public Hyperliquid wallets.

Questions:

- what are they betting on?
- where is their biggest risk?
- are they winning?
- if they lose, why might they lose?

### Day 4: user fear

Map operator fear into:

- sudden death
- slow bleed
- dying without understanding why

### Day 5: attribution

Break a result into:

- direction
- leverage / structure
- funding / fee drag
- execution / slippage
- timing

### Day 6: product fields

Convert analysis into product fields:

- risk fields
- cost fields
- attribution fields
- explanation fields

### Day 7: startup definition

Write the one-page startup definition:

- who is the user?
- what fear are we reducing?
- what is the first wedge?

## Plain-Language Product Framing

Do **not** say:

- we help you beat the market
- we are an AI trading analyst
- we are a public alpha feed

Do say:

- we show you which position is most dangerous
- we show you where profit is leaking
- we help you understand why the result looks wrong

## Founder Reminder

If the founder themselves would not pay for this workflow, or cannot explain why anyone would prefer it over existing public tools, that is a serious warning sign.
