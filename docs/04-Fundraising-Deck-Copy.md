# Dexy Fundraising Deck Copy

This is a draft narrative version of the fundraising deck.

## Slide 1: Dexy

**Dexy**  
The risk attribution and event alert layer for Hyperliquid operators

Dexy helps active onchain traders understand where risk is building, where profit is leaking, and why realized PnL diverges from intuition.

## Slide 2: Traders are not dying from lack of signal

The most painful operator problems on Hyperliquid are not:

- “I need one more signal”
- “I need one more AI chart”

They are:

- I didn’t realize this position was becoming dangerous
- I don’t know where my PnL is leaking
- I cannot explain why I made less than I expected

Hyperliquid users fear:

1. sudden death
2. slow bleed
3. dying without understanding why

## Slide 3: Why Hyperliquid, why now

Hyperliquid has become the most concentrated venue for serious onchain perp operators.

That matters because:

- operator pain is dense
- wallet and position state is sufficiently observable
- the ecosystem is active but fragmented

This creates an opening for a non-custodial control layer that does not need to become an exchange, a wallet, or a signal seller.

## Slide 4: What Dexy does

Dexy is a wallet-aware risk and attribution workflow.

It does not tell users what to buy.

It tells them:

- which position is most dangerous right now
- how much funding, fees, or execution drag is eating profit
- what happened and what to watch next

The first surfaces are:

- Telegram alerts
- minimal wallet dashboard

## Slide 5: Example user value

For an active Hyperliquid operator, Dexy can answer:

- Your SP500 short is now your highest-risk position
- Funding has become the second-largest source of loss
- Realized PnL is trailing intuition because direction was only part of the problem
- If you keep holding, the key risk to watch is liquidation distance rather than unrealized PnL

This turns fragmented data into action priority.

## Slide 6: First customer

Dexy’s first customer is not a beginner.

It is:

- an active Hyperliquid trader
- a small strategy operator
- a bot runner
- a builder-adjacent operator who has real notional exposure but no robust internal control tooling

These users do not need another signal source.

They need visibility, explanation, and prioritization.

## Slide 7: Why they will pay

Users do not pay Dexy for “AI.”

They pay if Dexy helps them:

- avoid a major mistake
- identify a dangerous position earlier
- understand why profit is being eroded
- reduce repetitive manual monitoring

This is a workflow product, not a content product.

## Slide 8: Market shape

There are already paid products proving:

- wallet analytics can be sold
- trader dashboards can be sold
- Hyperliquid-native intelligence can be sold

What has not been cleanly owned is the wallet-level wedge that combines:

- risk ranking
- attribution
- alerting
- explanation

for operators specifically.

## Slide 9: Competitive landscape

Today’s substitutes are fragmented:

- HyperTracker / CoinMarketMan for analytics
- Hyperdash for terminal and flow-linked trading surfaces
- Nansen for wallet intelligence
- native Hyperliquid interfaces for base trading

Dexy sits in the gap between generic analytics and execution tools:

- wallet-specific
- operator-first
- attribution-first
- alert-first

## Slide 10: Business model

Dexy starts with human-first SaaS:

- free
- pro
- operator / team

Then expands into:

- API
- webhooks
- MCP
- x402-compatible machine usage

The ceiling increases materially if Dexy becomes part of workflow and flow, not just analysis.

## Slide 11: Why we can win

Dexy does not need to out-data everyone.

It needs to out-workflow them.

The moat is:

- persistent use
- wallet-specific monitoring
- better attribution semantics
- action-prioritized explanation

This is defensible only if Dexy becomes a repeated operator habit, not a one-time dashboard.

## Slide 12: Roadmap

Phase 1:

- wallet input
- risk score
- liquidation-aware Telegram alerts
- funding / fee / PnL explanation

Phase 2:

- replay
- multi-wallet
- exports
- richer attribution

Phase 3:

- API
- MCP
- machine-native monetization

## Slide 13: Why now / why this team

The opportunity exists because Hyperliquid has already created the market density.

The right founder advantage here is not “best trader.”

It is:

- best at compressing fragmented operator pain into usable workflow
- best at turning public observability into clarity and action priority

## Slide 14: Raise / ask

We are raising to prove that wallet-level risk attribution and alerts can become a repeated, paid operator workflow on Hyperliquid.

The milestones are:

- productize the first wedge
- validate repeated usage
- validate payment for risk/attribution rather than signal
- expand into workflow and machine rails only after the human wedge is proven
